What is Koitus?

Koitus is a comprehensive social platform that combines dating, live streaming, content sharing, events, and community features in one seamless experience. Whether you're looking for romance, friendship, professional networking, or entertainment, Koitus provides the tools you need to connect authentically.

  • Smart matching algorithm
  • Live video streaming
  • Community forums and clubs
  • Events and meetups
  • Marketplace for creators
  • Privacy-first design
